Frequently Asked Questions

Is Xeyphr detectable by meeting software?

No. Xeyphr operates as a discrete desktop overlay utilizing OS-level hardware acceleration bypass. It does not show up in Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams screen shares.

How do credits work?

1 credit equals roughly 1,000 tokens of generation (combining context and output). Your credits never expire as long as your account is active.

Can I use it on Mac?

Currently, Xeyphr is optimized specifically for Windows 10/11 environments. Mac support is on our roadmap for Q4.
